Ethics & consent policy

Because this repository is public and intended to be cited, every case published here meets the same consent standard used by medical journals, not a lower internal-notes standard.

Before a case is published, an editor confirms:

  • Written informed consent has been obtained from the patient or guardian for any identifiable information or images, or the case has been fully de-identified so that consent is not required.
  • No patient name appears anywhere in the case.
  • Location is given only at district/state level — never village, ward, or exact address.
  • Any photos are cropped or blurred to remove identifying features.
  • Dates are given relative to the illness ("Day 3 of illness") rather than as calendar dates, where an exact date combined with a rare diagnosis and small location could identify the patient.
  • The signed consent form, where one exists, is filed in the private (non-public) consent folder — never attached to the public case page.

Why this matters

A case that will later be cited in a grant application or policy document needs to hold up to outside scrutiny. Treating consent and de-identification as a real editorial step — not a formality — is what makes that possible.
