Aims & scope

Field Notes in Medicine is a running repository of clinical cases and social-medical stories from rural practice — rare diseases, rare presentations of common diseases, and cases where a social or systemic factor shaped the outcome as much as the pathology did.

It exists to do three things at once: give our own team a durable, searchable reference for cases we've seen; give medical students real teaching material grounded in rural practice; and give us a citable, consistently structured record to draw on when writing grant applications or policy briefs.

How cases are structured

Every case follows the same template, adapted from the CARE guidelines used internationally for clinical case reports: patient context (never a name), presenting history, clinical and social context, findings, diagnosis, management, outcome, and a discussion of the learning point. Every case carries a permanent case ID, a submission date, and a set of tags so it can be found by specialty, disease, age group, setting, or the social determinant that shaped it.
