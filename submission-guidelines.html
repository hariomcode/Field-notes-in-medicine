Before you start

  • Have your de-identified images and lab reports ready to upload (no names, no visible faces unless consented and still de-identified elsewhere, no exact dates on scanned documents where avoidable).
  • Have written consent on file if the case includes anything identifiable — the form will ask you to confirm this.
  • Review the ethics & consent policy first if you're unsure whether de-identification is sufficient.

What the form will ask for

The form mirrors the CARE case-report structure: case type and tags, patient context (age group, sex, setting — never a name), presenting history, clinical and social context, findings, diagnosis, management, outcome and follow-up, and your own discussion of the learning point. There's also an optional field for the patient or family's own perspective, in their words, where relevant.

What happens after you submit

An editor reviews the case for clinical accuracy, de-identification, and tagging before it's published — usually within a couple of weeks. You'll be listed as the contributor on the published case.
